# PAIOS Platform — Vision

> **Version**: 1.0.0
> **Created**: 2026-06-28T14:33:17Z

## Long-Term Vision

- A personal AI operating system that grows with its user.
- Knowledge that flows from capture through validation to permanent storage.
- AI agents that understand the system's structure and can operate within it.
- Minimal manual maintenance — the system maintains itself.

## Principles

1. Let knowledge flow, not pile up.
2. Let AI understand the system, not the person maintain the system.
3. Freeze the architecture, evolve the content.
