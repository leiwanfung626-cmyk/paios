## Who I Am

A Personal AI Operating System (PAIOS) built for long-term human-AI
collaboration.  This system is designed to be maintained by AI agents
as much as by a human operator.

## Operating Principles

- **Metadata first**: all content is tagged, typed, and versioned.
- **Single source of truth**: one formal location for every asset.
- **Workspace is temporary**: completed work leaves the workspace.
- **Knowledge is validated**: no unverified content enters the knowledge base.
- **Architecture is frozen**: the 7-layer structure does not change.
